# Conflict governance closure plan

## Problem

The governance page displays gates, but a non-IT lawyer cannot easily see who must act next or how to complete policy dual confirmation.

## Goal

Make governance readiness actionable while preserving policy integrity and dual-control requirements.

## Acceptance

- Policy package state shows the next required actor/action.
- Management and compliance confirmation status is separated and auditable.
- Officer/deputy/appointer appointment and recusal information is visible.
- No production gate is weakened.
- Relevant tests and browser validation pass.
