# Small-firm operations readiness plan

## Problem

Health is available, but backup restore, incident response, upgrade, and rollback are unverified and not actionable.

## Goal

Provide a small-firm-friendly operations readiness surface and auditable evidence checklist.

## Acceptance

- Operators can see required evidence and current status.
- Backup/restore, incident owner, upgrade, and rollback have explicit states and next actions.
- Readiness does not claim success without evidence.
- Relevant tests and browser validation pass.
